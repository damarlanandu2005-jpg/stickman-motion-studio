# Fact-check notes (overnight run, 2026-11-16)
Sources: web search (Wikipedia, Guinness World Records, History.com, Britannica, news archives).
## db_cooper
- "nine years later" -> "in nineteen eighty": the cash was found Feb 1980, i.e. 8 years 2 months later.
- "only unsolved hijacking in American history" -> "in commercial aviation history" (the wording used by sources).
- "freezing rain" -> "rain" (unverified as freezing precipitation).
## jungle_fall
- Boat and hut were found on day TEN, loggers the NEXT day (day 11). Script said day eleven for both. Fixed ("ten days", "tenth day").
- Injuries: collarbone, swollen eye, deep cuts (calf/arm) - "cut on her arm" alone was incomplete.
- "only one who came home" -> "only one who survived" (her mother survived the fall, died later).
## lightning_man
- "knocked a nail out of his boot" was wrong: the 1942 strike blew off his big toenail (and burned his leg).
- "Guinness called him the human lightning rod" -> Guinness recognises the record; the nickname came from the public.
- "died of something far more ordinary" was misleading (he died by suicide in 1983). Replaced with the plain fact: died in 1983 aged 71; cause left out on purpose.
- Wife struck once while hanging washing in the yard, Roy beside her: kept, reworded to "in the yard beside him".
