s1: How does a mechanical clock tick?
s2: Inside is a spring, pulling a stack of gears.
s3: If nothing stopped it, they would spin free in seconds.
s4: The trick is a small part called the escapement.
s5: A toothed wheel wants to turn, but an anchor-shaped piece catches one tooth, and holds it.
s6: Then a swinging pendulum rocks the anchor sideways, releasing the tooth.
s7: The wheel jumps forward one step, and the other end of the anchor catches the next tooth.
s8: Tick. Rock back, tock.
s9: Each swing lets exactly one tooth through, and the pendulum's length sets how long a swing takes.
s10: The spring supplies the push.
s11: The pendulum supplies the patience.
