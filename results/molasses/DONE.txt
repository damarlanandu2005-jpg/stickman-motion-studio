Shot 1 (hook): Close up of brown syrup dripping, matching the year 1919 hook.
Shot 2 (wave): Camera pulls back to a wide street view with calm people and "21" appearing in the sky.
Shot 3 (tank): Low angle looking up at the huge 15-meter steel tank with steam and tiny workers.
Shot 4 (neighborhood): High aerial orbit over the busy neighborhood and train track.
Shot 5 (built): Medium shot of the tank under construction with scaffold, sparks, and hammering workers.
Shot 6 (groan): Low angle of the tank bulging and rippling, with surprised kids looking up on a warm day.
Shot 7 (rivets): Extreme close up of the tank wall as rivets pop like bullets, spraying brown syrup.
Shot 8 (burst): A snap-zoom out with a flash and big shake as the tank tears open and debris flies.
Shot 9 (wave): The camera retreats fast in front of a rolling 8-meter molasses wave chasing a horse and workers.
Shot 10 (flatten): Side view of the wave bending the elevated train track and lifting a shocked fireman.
Shot 11 (clean): Dawn shot of tired workers scrubbing the sticky brown street while water splashes.
Shot 12 (smell): The modern street with a park and plaque; a man sniffs a sweet heart-shaped smell.
