# The Pig War: narration and shot plan (copyable template)

Video: 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps, 43.17 s (1295 frames). Frame number / 30 = seconds.

---

## 1. NARRATION (word for word)

In eighteen fifty-nine, two empires nearly went to war over one pig.
It lived on San Juan Island, where nobody agreed which country owned the land. One morning, it wandered into an American farmer's potato garden. Again. So he shot it.
The pig's British owner demanded a hundred dollars. The farmer offered ten. Soon came threats of arrest, soldiers, and warships.
Both sides camped on opposite ends of the island, ordered never to fire first. They waited twelve years.
Finally, a German emperor drew the border. The island went to America. And the only casualty of the Pig War... was the pig.

Structure: HOOK (the absurd claim) > SETUP (place) > INCIDENT (3 beats) > ESCALATION (money, then threats) > STANDOFF > TIME JUMP > RESOLUTION > TWIST LINE (the last 3 words land on the visual punchline).

## 2. TEMPLATE: copy this block once per shot

```
SHOT #__  name: ______   frames __–__ (sec __–__)
WORDS:      "exact phrase spoken in this shot"
PLACE:      where it is, what is in the frame
LIGHT:      day | dawn | golden | night | indoor | cycle (time-lapse)
CAMERA:     start framing > end framing; move (push-in / pull-back / orbit / static); lens (wide = fov 45-50, tight = fov 22-34); roll tilt; camera kick on impact
CHARACTERS: who | outfit | pose (arms) | face | facing / position
EFFECTS:    2D cards (bubbles, smoke, stars, steam), flashes, props that change
SFX:        what you hear, and on which frame
MUSIC:      what the bed does
RULE:       the picture reaction lands 0–3 frames BEFORE its word
```

Rules I followed for the whole video:
- Never edit or re-time the voice. The shots are cut to the voice, not the other way round.
- No captions on screen. Text only if it exists in the world (the "$100" and "$10" bubbles).
- Original characters only (no real people, no mascots).
- Audio: music at 50% of the voice level (-6 dB), SFX at 50% or less, no ducking, one overall gain to about -14 LUFS plus a -1 dBFS limiter.

Cast key (all are simple stick-body characters with a round head, drawn as flat cards in a 3D world):
- **Pig**: pink side view, curly tail, chews a potato; KO = on its back with X eyes, halo, stars.
- **Farmer** (US): blue tunic, yellow hair, no hat.
- **Brit** (pig owner): tall black top hat, dark green coat, red face when angry.
- **US soldier**: blue coat, dark shako hat, musket. **UK soldier**: red coat, tall shako, musket.
- **Old soldiers** (after the time jump): same uniforms, long white beards.
- **Emperor**: spiked pickelhaube helmet, blue coat, cape, white beard.
- **Guards**: stern, flank the throne.

Tips for your own story:
1. Keep one visual idea per sentence; cut on the sentence break, not mid-phrase.
2. Give each shot one clear camera move (push-in, pull-back, or snap-zoom) and one change in the characters' faces.
3. Put the loudest sound on the key action (the gunshot) and silence the music just before it.
4. Save the biggest visual (the statue and halo) for the last three words.
