CAST:
- Woman (Troffea): red dress, white cap, brown hair, barefoot.
- Dancers: medieval peasants in brown, grey, green clothes, many sizes, all barefoot.
- Councilman: tall black hat, long purple robe, gold chain, grey beard.
- Musicians: three men in red caps with a drum, a flute, a horn.

SHOT 1 (hook)
WORDS: "In fifteen eighteen,"
PLACE: narrow medieval town street, timber houses, cobblestones, a well.
LIGHT: dawn (pale yellow sky).
CAMERA: low and tight on the woman's bare feet, then rise to her face, fov 36 > 32.
CHARACTERS: Woman standing still, face = blank, eyes wide.
EFFECTS: morning mist at the ground.
SFX: a single church bell. MUSIC: none.

SHOT 2 (dance)
WORDS: "a woman stepped into a street in France and began to dance."
PLACE: same street, wider.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: pull back to wide (fov 32 > 42); small tilt left and right with her dance.
CHARACTERS: Woman dances alone: arms up, swinging, body tilts; people at windows watch, face = confused.
EFFECTS: dust at her feet.
SFX: soft steps. MUSIC: a single plucked string starts to play a dance beat.

SHOT 3 (days)
WORDS: "She did not stop for days."
PLACE: same street, the sun and moon moving across the sky.
LIGHT: cycle (day to night to day).
CAMERA: static wide, fov 42.
CHARACTERS: Woman keeps dancing; hair messy, face = tired but still moving.
EFFECTS: sky cycle three times, stars at night.
SFX: steps fade out and in. MUSIC: plucked beat continues.

SHOT 4 (join)
WORDS: "Within a week, thirty-four people had joined her."

LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: high crane, wide, slow orbit right, fov 46.
CHARACTERS: About thirty peasants dancing, same loose rhythm, faces = blank.
EFFECTS: dust cloud, leaves.
SFX: many steps. MUSIC: adds a drum.

SHOT 5 (four hundred)

LIGHT: golden.
CAMERA: very high, top-down, static, then slow zoom out; fov 40 > 54.
CHARACTERS: A huge crowd of dancers (at least one hundred in view) all swaying.

SHOT 6 (nobody)

SHOT 7 (cure)

SHOT 8 (stage)

LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: wide pull-back, fov 40 > 48.
