In fifteen eighteen, a woman stepped into a street in France and began to dance. She did not stop for days. Within a week, thirty-four people had joined her. Soon there were four hundred. Nobody knew why. The city leaders decided the cure was more dancing. They built a stage and hired musicians. It only made things worse. People danced until their feet bled. Some collapsed and never got up. Then, one day, the music stopped. And so did the dancing. To this day, nobody knows why.
