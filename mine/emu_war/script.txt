In nineteen thirty-two, the Australian army went to war against birds. And lost. Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat. So soldiers arrived with machine guns and a truck. The emus split into small groups and ran in every direction. The truck could not catch them. The guns could not hit them. After a month, the army gave up and went home. The birds were still there. Today the emus are still eating wheat. The final score was birds one, army zero.
