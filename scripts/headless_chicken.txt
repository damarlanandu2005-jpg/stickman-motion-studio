TITLE: Mike the Headless Chicken

NARRATION:
In nineteen forty-five, a farmer cut off a chicken's head. The chicken refused to die.
On a farm in Colorado, Lloyd Olsen swung the axe to make dinner. But the blade missed, leaving most of the brain stem and one ear behind.
The rooster, named Mike, stood back up. He tried to peck. He tried to crow. He kept walking around the yard like nothing had happened.
So the farmer kept him. He fed Mike milk and grain straight down the open neck with an eyedropper.
Mike didn't last a day. He lasted eighteen months. He grew from two pounds to eight. He toured the country as a sideshow, earning thousands of dollars a month.
Doctors said it should have been impossible.
In the end, Mike didn't die from the axe. He choked, one night, in a motel room, months after he should have been dinner.
