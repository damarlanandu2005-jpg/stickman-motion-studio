TITLE: The Pig War
LENGTH: 40 seconds

NARRATION:
In eighteen fifty nine, America and Britain almost started a war over one pig.
Both countries claimed the same tiny island between Canada and the United States.
One morning, an American farmer found a British pig eating his potatoes.
So he shot it.
He offered the owner ten dollars. The British owner wanted one hundred.
Soon soldiers arrived. Then warships. Then cannons, all pointed at each other.
Hundreds of men waited for the first shot.
But nobody fired. Officers on both sides refused to fight over a pig.
The island was finally settled by a German emperor, twelve years later.
The only casualty of the whole war was the pig.
