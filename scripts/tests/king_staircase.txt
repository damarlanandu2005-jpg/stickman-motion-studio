TITLE: The Staircase That Bankrupted a King

NARRATION:
A king wanted to build something nobody had ever seen before.
He summoned the finest builder in the kingdom.
His request was simple.
Build me a staircase that will make everyone jealous.
The builder began constructing a magnificent tower and staircase.
But the king wanted more.
He demanded expensive materials and extravagant details.
The palace filled with workers, craftsmen and royal guests.
The costs kept rising.
Even the materials had to be specially prepared.
The staircase became more expensive with every step.
Soon, the kingdom's money began disappearing.
The king had spent a fortune on one staircase.
And his treasury was nearly empty.
His greatest masterpiece had become his biggest financial disaster.
