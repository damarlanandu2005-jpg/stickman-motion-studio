TITLE: The Emu War

NARRATION:
In nineteen thirty-two, the Australian army went to war against birds. And lost.
Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat. So soldiers arrived with machine guns and a truck.
The emus split into small groups and ran in every direction. The truck could not catch them. The guns could not hit them.
After a month, the army gave up and went home. The birds were still there.
Today the emus are still eating wheat. The final score was birds one, army zero.

SHOTS:
CAST:
- Emu: tall, grey-brown shaggy body, long thin neck, long legs, small head, orange eye.
- Soldier: khaki uniform, wide-brim hat folded up on one side, rifle or machine gun.
- Farmer: straw hat, brown overalls, white shirt.

SHOT 1 (hook)
WORDS: "In nineteen thirty-two,"
PLACE: dusty red outback road, dry fence, one dead tree.
LIGHT: golden (low orange sun).
CAMERA: low and tight on a soldier's boots marching, then tilt up to his face, fov 38 > 34.
CHARACTERS: Soldier, tall, khaki, wide hat, face = serious.
EFFECTS: dust puffs at each step.
SFX: marching boots. MUSIC: military snare drum starts.

SHOT 2 (war)
WORDS: "the Australian army went to war against birds. And lost."
PLACE: wide wheat field, a big army truck, a row of soldiers, and a line of emus facing them.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: pull back to a wide shot (fov 34 > 46); hard snap-zoom to one emu on the word "lost".
CHARACTERS: Six soldiers in a line, rifles up; five emus facing them, necks high, eye = calm. On "lost" the emu turns its head and looks at the camera.
EFFECTS: wheat swaying.
SFX: whoosh on pull-back, one silly honk on "lost". MUSIC: snare continues, then trumpet slides down.

SHOT 3 (wheat)
WORDS: "Twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat."
PLACE: huge golden wheat field to the horizon, fence and a small farmhouse.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: high crane, wide, slow push-in, fov 48 > 44.
CHARACTERS: Farmer on the left, hat in hands, face = desperate. A huge crowd of emus (at least forty) eat wheat and bob their heads.
EFFECTS: wheat bits flying from the beaks.
SFX: crunching, crowd of low emu drums. MUSIC: continues.

SHOT 4 (guns)
WORDS: "So soldiers arrived with machine guns and a truck."

LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: low side tracking shot, moving right, fov 40.

EFFECTS: big dust cloud behind the truck.
SFX: engine, whoosh, metal clank. MUSIC: continues, brass fanfare.

SHOT 5 (split)
WORDS: "The emus split into small groups and ran in every direction."
PLACE: wheat field, view from above.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: high top-down, static, fov 50.
CHARACTERS: One big group of emus bursts into six small groups running in six directions; legs move fast; soldiers stand in the middle, heads turning left and right, face = confused.
EFFECTS: dust trails behind each emu, sweat drop on one soldier.
SFX: burst of footsteps, comic spring sound. MUSIC: fast chase music starts.

SHOT 6 (truck)
WORDS: "The truck could not catch them."
PLACE: dirt road with wheat on both sides.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: side tracking shot following an emu running fast, fov 36.
CHARACTERS: One emu running at full speed in front; the truck behind it, bouncing; soldiers hold their hats; the emu looks back, eye = smug.
EFFECTS: dust, bouncing truck, flying hat.
SFX: engine strain, emu whoop. MUSIC: chase continues.

SHOT 7 (miss)
WORDS: "The guns could not hit them."
PLACE: open field with a sandy ridge behind.
LIGHT: day.
CAMERA: wide, static, shake on each shot, fov 44.
CHARACTERS: Soldier at the gun, face = angry, firing; bullets kick up sand far behind; emus zigzag and are safe; one emu does a little hop.

SHOT 8 (month)
WORDS: "After a month, the army gave up and went home."

LIGHT: dawn (grey-pink sky).
CAMERA: wide pull-back, slow, fov 40 > 48.
CHARACTERS: Soldiers sit slumped in the truck, hats down, face = sad. Calendar page flips (text: 30 days) drawn in the sky.
EFFECTS: dust trail, falling calendar pages.
SFX: page flips, sad trombone. MUSIC: slow minor melody.

PLACE: big scoreboard on a post standing in the wheat (text: BIRDS 1 - ARMY 0).
LIGHT: golden.
CAMERA: slow push-in to the scoreboard, biggest picture on the last three words, fov 46 > 36.

SFX: crowd cheer, final honk on "zero". MUSIC: victory trumpet.
