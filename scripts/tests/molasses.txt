TITLE: The Great Molasses Flood

NARRATION:
In nineteen nineteen, a wave of sweet brown goo killed twenty-one people in Boston.
A huge tank held over two million gallons of molasses. It stood in the middle of a busy neighborhood. It was built fast, and it was never tested.
On a warm January afternoon, the tank groaned. Rivets popped like bullets. Then it burst.
A wave eight meters tall rushed down the street at fifty kilometers an hour. It flattened trains, houses, and horses.
It took weeks to clean. And on hot days, locals say the area still smells sweet.
