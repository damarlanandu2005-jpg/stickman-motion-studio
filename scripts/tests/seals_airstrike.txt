TITLE: Why These SEALs Called an Airstrike on THEMSELVES

Their own team was caught in the middle of the attack.
But they had no other choice.
The strike hit.
And somehow, they survived.
A helicopter arrived to get them out.
But the mission nearly cost them everything.
