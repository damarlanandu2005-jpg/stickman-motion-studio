TITLE: He Defused 41 Bombs Without Seeing COLOR

NARRATION:
Arthur was colorblind.
But that didn't stop him from becoming a bomb disposal expert.
He was called whenever a dangerous device needed to be defused.
One day, he faced a bomb with only seconds remaining.
The instructions depended on identifying colored wires.
But Arthur couldn't see the difference between them.
He had to find another way.
Instead of guessing, he carefully studied the circuit.
He traced every wire and followed its connections.
There were six wires, and one wrong cut could kill him.
The countdown kept ticking.
Everyone was waiting for an answer.
Then Arthur noticed something others had missed.
He understood the circuit.
And he defused the bomb.
He had done it again.
Forty-one bombs, without relying on color.
