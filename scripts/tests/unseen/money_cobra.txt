Delhi, under British rule, had too many venomous cobras. The government had a clever idea: pay a bounty for every dead snake. At first it worked, and people brought in cobras by the hundreds. Then officials noticed something odd. The bounty office kept getting cobras, but the streets seemed no safer. Some people had begun breeding cobras at home, just to collect the reward. When the government found out, it cancelled the bounty. The breeders now had thousands of worthless snakes, so they let them go. Delhi ended up with more cobras than before. Pay people for a result, and some will make more of the problem.
