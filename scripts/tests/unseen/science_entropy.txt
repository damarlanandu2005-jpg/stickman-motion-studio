Your coffee goes cold, but it never heats itself back up. Why? Picture a cup with a billion tiny balls bouncing inside. Hot means the balls move fast. When they hit the cooler air, they pass their speed along. Soon the air balls bounce a little faster, and the coffee balls a little slower. Could it run backwards? Yes, in theory. Every single bounce is reversible. But for the room to hand its energy back, countless random bounces would have to line up perfectly at once. Count the ways energy can spread out, and spreading wins by a number with more zeros than there are stars. So nothing forbids a warm cup. It is just absurdly unlikely. That is entropy: not a force, just the odds.
