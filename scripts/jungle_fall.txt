TITLE: The Girl Who Fell From the Sky

NARRATION:
A seventeen-year-old fell two miles out of the sky, and walked out of the jungle eleven days later.
On Christmas Eve, Juliane's plane flew into a thunderstorm over the Amazon. Lightning struck. The plane broke apart in the air.
Still strapped to her seat, she fell two miles into the rainforest. When she woke, she was alone on the jungle floor. Ninety-one other people had died. She had only a broken collarbone and a cut on her arm.
Her parents were biologists. She remembered one thing her father taught her: find running water, and follow it downstream. Water leads to people.
So she waded through rivers for eleven days. She ran out of food. Maggots burrowed into her wounds. She passed the bodies of other passengers.
On the eleventh day, she found a small boat and a hut. Loggers found her there the next morning.
Out of ninety-two people, she was the only one who came home.
