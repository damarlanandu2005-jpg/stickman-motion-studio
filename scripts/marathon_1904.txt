TITLE: The Worst Olympic Race Ever

NARRATION:
The nineteen-oh-four Olympic marathon was the most disastrous race in history.
It was run in ninety-degree heat, on dusty roads, behind cars that kicked up clouds of dirt. There was only one water stop in the entire race, on purpose. The organizer wanted to study dehydration.
One runner collapsed, caught a ride in a car for eleven miles, then jogged into the stadium and was declared the winner. Until the car was found.
The real winner, Thomas Hicks, was kept moving by his coaches, who fed him brandy mixed with rat poison as a stimulant. He finished hallucinating, and had to be carried across the line.
A Cuban mailman ran in street clothes and dress shoes, stopped to eat rotten apples from an orchard, got a stomach ache, took a nap, and still finished fourth.
Of the thirty-two who started, only fourteen finished.
