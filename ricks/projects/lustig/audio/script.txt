A man sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice. In nineteen twenty-five, he invited scrap dealers to a Paris hotel, posing as a government official. He told them the tower was rusting, too costly to repair, and must be sold for scrap. A dealer named Andre Poisson bought it. You see, the tower was never his to sell, but Poisson was too embarrassed to call the police. So six months later, the con man came back, found new dealers, and sold the Eiffel Tower again. This time, one of them called the police. He fled the country, and the tower is still standing.
