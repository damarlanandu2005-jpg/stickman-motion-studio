A seventeen-year-old fell two miles out of the sky, and walked out of the jungle eleven days later. On Christmas Eve, Juliane's plane flew into a thunderstorm over the Peruvian Amazon. Lightning struck. The plane broke apart in the air. Still strapped to her seat, she fell two miles into the rainforest. When she woke, she was alone on the jungle floor. Ninety-one other people had died. She had a broken collarbone, a swollen eye, and deep cuts. Her parents were biologists. She remembered one thing her father taught her: find running water, and follow it downstream. Water leads to people. So she followed the water for ten days. She ran out of food. Maggots burrowed into her wounds. She passed the bodies of other passengers. On the tenth day, she found a small boat and a hut. Loggers found her there the next day. Out of ninety-two people, she was the only one who survived.
